# Security

If you've found a security issue in one of our repositories, thank you. We take these seriously, and we'd rather hear about them early.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Instead:

1. Go to the **Security** tab of the affected repository.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what impact you think it has.

If that option isn't available, get in touch through the email address of this organisation and we'll arrange a private channel.

## What happens next

We'll acknowledge your report within a few working days, keep you informed as we look into it, and let you know once it's resolved. If you'd like credit for the find, we're happy to give it.

## Scope

This policy covers the public repositories in this organisation. Most of what's here is shared as it is, so fixes are made to the latest version only.

Please give us reasonable time to put things right before disclosing anything publicly. We'll do the same for you.
