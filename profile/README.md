# Don't Panic

**Modern IT for people, not just machines.**

Don't Panic is a consultancy, led by Eelco Meuter, for organisations wrestling with large, complex technology problems. Twenty years of this work have taught us one thing above all: the hard part is rarely the technology. It's how people use it, rebuild it, and explain it to one another.

So we keep things calm, and we keep them simple.

## How we work

1. **Listen.** We start with your team, not your tech stack.
2. **Simplify.** We strip away what isn't needed, until a system feels natural to use.
3. **Deliver.** Real-world impact is the measure that matters. The rest is detail.

## What you'll find here

Tools, experiments and notes that come out of our work, shared as they are. If something proves useful, or could be better, do open an issue. We'd be glad to hear from you.
